Flow Canvas Concepts

Three screens for the Business flows tab, drawn as if the canvas were the editor: drag boxes between lanes, type on the diagram, drop sticky notes, mark decisions, export. Pick a skin to see the same screens in another look.

1. Edit on the canvas

Weekly payroll, with the Review OT step being dragged into the IT lane. Nothing opens a form for this.

  1. 1Select a step to get a small toolbar and handles. Double-click to type, Delete to remove.
  2. 2Drag to another lane. The lane lights up and the drop changes the team. Order and spacing follow on their own.
  3. 3Sticky notes sit anywhere. Free text, no fields, saved with the flow.

2. Type to build

Double-click an empty spot in a lane and start typing. Enter finishes the step and starts the next, already linked.

  1. 1Inline text box on the lane. The team is the lane you clicked in.
  2. 2Enter adds the next step to the right with an arrow. Esc stops.
  3. 3Paste several lines and each becomes a step. Fix the details later.

3. Explain it, then share it

Quote approval with start and end markers, a yes/no decision, labelled branches, and the export menu.

  1. 1Start and end pills show where the process begins and finishes.
  2. 2Decision diamond with labelled branches. "Changes needed" stays as the dashed send-back arrow.
  3. 3Export as PNG or SVG for a slide or chat. Mermaid stays for docs.

Suggested skin plan

Paper as the default light theme and Blueprint as its dark counterpart, so the canvas follows the app's light and dark mode. Marker is an optional "sketch" skin for workshops, where a rough look invites people to change things.

Same data, same layout in all three. Only colour, line weight, corner radius and the step font change.
