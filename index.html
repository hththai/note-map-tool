    A ">" line starts a section, and the "-" lines under it belong to it. An indented line under a section or item is its note: tap the card on the map to read it. Criticality is high, medium or low. A link whose note contains "manual" is drawn dashed. Names in Links must match a sheet or app name.

  Indented line = note for the step above

        "<- Team: message" means the step starts when that team sends the message. "<- 2" waits on step 2 itself, and a step can have several (<- 1 | <- 2). A step with no "<-" follows the step before it, and "<- start" gives it no arrow in. "-> 3: message" draws a dashed arrow back to step 3. Editing with the form rewrites this text, so comments you add here are not kept. Drag a step to another lane or place to move it.
